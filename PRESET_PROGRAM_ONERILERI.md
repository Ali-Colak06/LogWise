# Preset Program Onerileri

Bu belge, LogWise icin onerilen yeni preset programlari toplu halde incelemek icin hazirlandi.

## 1. Etiket Onerileri

| Alan | Onerilen degerler | Amac |
|---|---|---|
| `level` | `beginner`, `intermediate`, `advanced` | Zorluk filtresi |
| `environment` | `home`, `gym`, `hybrid` | Ev / salon ayrimi |
| `goal` | `strength`, `hypertrophy`, `general-fitness`, `fat-loss`, `skill`, `endurance` | Program amaci |
| `equipment` | `bodyweight`, `chair`, `bar`, `dumbbell`, `machine`, `full-gym` | Gerekli ekipman |
| `daysPerWeek` | `2`, `3`, `4`, `5`, `6` | Haftalik gun sayisi |
| `focus` | `full-body`, `upper-lower`, `push-up`, `pull-up`, `core`, `lower-body`, `conditioning` | Ana vurgu |

| Key | Program Adi | Seviye | Ortam | Hedef | Gun | Yapi |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `home-full-body-3-beginner` | Evde Full Body Baslangic | beginner | home | general-fitness | 3 | Full body |
| `home-upper-lower-4-beginner` | Evde Upper/Lower Baslangic | beginner | home | general-fitness | 4 | Upper/Lower |
| `bodyweight-strength-3` | Bodyweight Strength Foundation | beginner | home | strength | 3 | Full body |
| `calisthenics-beginner-3` | Calisthenics Baslangic | beginner | home | skill | 3 | Skill + temel kuvvet |
| `push-up-focus-3` | Push-Up Gelisim Programi | beginner | home | skill | 3 | Push odakli |
| `pull-up-focus-3` | Pull-Up Gelisim Programi | intermediate | home | skill | 3 | Pull odakli |
| `home-hypertrophy-4` | Evde Hipertrofi | intermediate | home | hypertrophy | 4 | Upper/Lower |
